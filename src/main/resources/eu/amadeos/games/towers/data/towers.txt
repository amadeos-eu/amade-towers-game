# The towers of Towers. Each block is one stage of a tower; the first stage of a kind is built, upgrade
# names the stage it becomes, and "like" starts a stage with everything of the one before.
#
#   shot     arrow: flies after its target and hits it
#            ball:  flies to where its target will be and bursts there (splash: the radius)
#            frost: a wave of cold around the tower; units keep the part slow of their speed for cold seconds
#   cost     gold to build, or to upgrade to this stage
#   range    in tiles, from the middle of the tower
#   reload   seconds between two shots
#   damage   hit points per hit, less the target's armour
#   speed    tiles per second the shot flies
#   turn     radians per second the turret turns (6 if not given)

tower archer

  range 3.0
